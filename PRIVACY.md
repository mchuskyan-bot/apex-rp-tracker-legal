# Privacy Policy – Apex RP Tracker

**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Apex RP Tracker ("the Bot") is a community-created Discord bot designed to track Apex Legends Ranked Points (RP), display player statistics, and provide leaderboards.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot collects, how it is used, and how it is stored.

## 2. Information We Collect
When you use the Bot, the following information may be collected and stored:

- **Discord User ID:** Used to identify your Discord account.
- **Apex Legends Username:** Used to identify your Apex Legends profile.
- **Gaming Platform:** Your selected platform (PC, PlayStation, Xbox, or Nintendo Switch).
- **Ranked Points (RP):** Your submitted RP totals and recorded gains or losses.
- **Tracking History:** Records of your RP changes and leaderboard statistics.

The Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, or personal account login credentials.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
Collected information is used exclusively to:
- Track individual players' RP changes.
- Calculate RP gains and losses.
- Maintain server leaderboards.
- Display ranked statistics to participating Discord server members.
- Provide the Bot's core functionality.

## 4. Data Storage and Security
Information is stored using the Bot's database and hosting environment.

The Bot administrators take reasonable steps to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no system can guarantee complete security.

## 5. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade user information.

Information may be displayed to members of Discord servers using the Bot through leaderboards and RP announcements.

If third-party statistics services are integrated, relevant information may be processed by those services according to their own privacy policies.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the Bot's functionality.

Users may request deletion of their stored information by contacting the Bot or Discord server administrators.

Where available, the Bot's account removal command may also be used to unlink a player's profile.

## 7. Children's Privacy
The Bot is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children under 13. Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws.

## 8. Third-Party Services
Apex RP Tracker is not affiliated with Electronic Arts, Respawn Entertainment, or Discord.

The Bot may interact with third-party services to retrieve or process game-related statistics. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot's features and functionality change.

Continued use of the Bot after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 10. Contact Information
For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or concerns about stored information, contact the Discord server administrators.
